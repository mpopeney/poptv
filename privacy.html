Pop TV

Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Pop TV is a private, home-made DVR used by a single household. It runs on a computer in that home. This policy explains what it does with information, including the Google account access it requests.

What Pop TV collects

Nothing is collected about anyone. Pop TV has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts with us, and no servers of ours. Everything it keeps (recordings, what each family member is watching, their settings) stays on the family's own computer and in the family's own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

When the family connects Pop TV to their Google Drive, it asks only for the drive.file permission: access to the files Pop TV itself creates, and nothing else in the Drive. Pop TV uses that access only to store its own recordings and backups in a folder it creates, and to read them back for playback or to restore the system.

  • Files are encrypted on the family's computer before they are uploaded.
  • Pop TV can't see, read, change, or delete any other files in the Drive.
  • Nothing from Google Drive is shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising.
  • Pop TV's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The files Pop TV created stay in the Drive until they are deleted.

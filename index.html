Pop TV

A home DVR for antenna TV

Pop TV is a private, home-made system that records free over-the-air television and plays it on the TVs in one family's home, with a program guide, recordings, and a few extras like public-domain classic films and podcasts.

It isn't a public service: there's nothing to sign up for or download here. This page exists so Google can show who is asking when the family connects Pop TV to their own Google Drive.

Google Drive

Pop TV can use the family's own Google Drive to store its recordings and backups. It asks only for access to the files it creates itself, and encrypts them before they leave the house. It can't see anything else in Drive. See the privacy policy.

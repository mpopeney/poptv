Pop TV

Terms

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Pop TV is a private system made for one household's own use. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody outside that household can sign up for or use it. It's provided as is, with no warranty.

It records free over-the-air broadcasts for the household's personal viewing, and plays public-domain and openly licensed material from the Internet Archive and public podcast feeds.

See the privacy policy for how it handles information and Google Drive access.
